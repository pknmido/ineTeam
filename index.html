What ineTeam does

We built this because organizing games at INPT was difficult. Now it's not.

🏟️

Any sport, any time

Football, basketball, volleyball, table tennis — pick your sport, pick your time, and see who shows up.

🤝

No more begging in groups

Find players who actually want to play, add them, and build your go-to squad.

💬

Chat without the noise

Talk, plan, and play right inside the app. No WhatsApp groups, no phone numbers needed.

📈

Know your level

Rate yourself 10–100 per sport. See where you're at, and watch yourself improve every game.
